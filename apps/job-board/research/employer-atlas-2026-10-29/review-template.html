The investment is your time

Follow real bottlenecks. Diligence the team.

My thesis favors science businesses solving expensive problems for identifiable buyers: electricity, grid equipment, nuclear fuel, industrial heat, semiconductor production, scientific tools and useful AI deployment.

A growing business, a good employer and the right role are three separate questions. Funding, a famous investor, a popular product or a high stock price cannot answer all three.

40% · Established operating businesses

Start with public reporting, actual customers and a specific business unit. Still investigate reorganizations, manager quality and workplace demands.

35% · Commercializing growth companies

Look for customer proof, a financed milestone and transferable skills. Ask what happens if the next round or project slips.

25% · Frontier bets

Fusion, new reactors, space and robotics can offer meaningful work. Technical, regulatory and financing risk should be a conscious choice.

Known, unknown, and worth asking

What the research can — and can’t — tell us.

There is no comparable employer-level boomerang rate in this screen. Most employers also lack comparable public tenure or voluntary-retention data. A return story is a lead; a rate requires a denominator, a period and a distinction between voluntary departures and layoffs.

How to assess pride and return behavior

  1. Ask for team-level voluntary attrition, internal promotions and voluntary boomerang hires, with definitions and periods.
  2. Separate laid-off rehires and check whether returners stay at least 12 months.
  3. Speak to current and former colleagues in the relevant function. Ask why the last people left, what happens after a missed goal and whether leave is usable.
  4. Treat review sites and awards as question generators. Favor specific, recent, corroborated examples over star averages.

A source hierarchy with limits

Use filings, official notices and dated employer policies for factual terms; use independent reporting to challenge the employer narrative; use relevant employee conversations to investigate the actual team. Company claims are labeled as claims. An unobserved event is unknown.

The search ledger contains discovery leads, including unrelated namesakes and rejected ATS results. Those results are not evidence unless explicitly attached to the employer. Career-page access does not establish financial health, permission to redistribute jobs or successful API integration.

All source connectors remain unapproved and disabled. Five industry collections plus public/remote tags are a proposed taxonomy; the current job-board application still uses its original three industries.
